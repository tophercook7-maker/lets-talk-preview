Let's Talk offers coaching, mentoring and advisory conversations. Sessions are booked and paid for in advance and take place online — by text chat, voice, or video, whichever you prefer.

What this is not. I am a coach and a friendly, experienced listener — not a licensed therapist, counsellor, physician, lawyer or financial adviser.
