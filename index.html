Before you reach out

How I work.

No programme, no packages you have to grow into. Just a few things I hold to, every single time.

One

It stays between us

What you say isn't repeated, shared, or brought up later — not to anyone, not even as an anonymous example. That's the whole basis of it.

Two

Zero judgment, genuinely

No lectures, no "I told you so," no face you have to manage. You can tell me the unflattering version — that's usually the useful one.

Three

I'll be honest with you

Kind isn't the same as agreeable. If I think you're talking yourself out of something, I'll say so — gently, and then I'll listen to why.

Four

You don't have to arrive organised

No intake form, no worksheets, no homework before we start. "I don't really know how to explain it" is a fine opening line.

Five

No pressure to continue

Come once. Come every week. Come back in a year when things get loud again. There's no package you're failing to complete.

Six

I'm not a therapist — and I'll say so

I'm a coach and a friendly, experienced ear. If what you're carrying needs clinical support, I'll tell you honestly and help you find the right person.

Honest answers

The things people ask me quietly.

What if I get emotional?

Then you get emotional. It's a conversation, not a performance — and you'd be surprised how often it happens about five minutes in. I'm not fazed, and I won't make it a thing.

Do I need to know what I want to say first?

No. Plenty of people start with "I don't really know why I booked this." That's usually a sign it was the right call.

Can I keep it anonymous?

Your first name is plenty. You never have to tell me where you work or who anyone is — say "my boss" and "my friend" and we'll get along fine.

What if it's not for me?

Then tell me, honestly, and that's the end of it — no hard feelings and no follow-up emails trying to change your mind.

Is 30 minutes really enough?

It's enough to get clear on one thing and leave with a next step. Most people are surprised how much lifts just from saying it out loud to someone who isn't involved.

How soon can we talk?

Often the same day. If I'm free you'll see it on the calendar — and if it's urgent, say so when you book and I'll do my best to move things.
